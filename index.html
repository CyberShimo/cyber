About

Shimo and Claude (Anthropic's AI) built these together. Four apps so far: two that clean out a phone and a PC, one that keeps a PC's memory under control, and one that builds Android APKs entirely from your phone using GitHub.

Every one of them started as a problem worth solving rather than a product idea. Most cleaners on the market invent junk that isn't there, hide behind fake scan counters, and then ask for money. These don't. If nothing is found, they say nothing was found.

They're free, they always will be, and they're still being worked on. Bug reports are genuinely useful — the beta label is honest, not a disclaimer.

On trusting a stranger's cleaner

I know there are websites and apps that look like mine, say they clean your phone, and look one hundred percent legitimate — and then scam you or hand you a virus. I've been on the other side of that. I went looking for a cleaner like the one I ended up building, and I came away with a virus on my phone instead.

So I get it if you don't want to install this. I get it if you don't trust me. There's no reason you should, and I'm not going to try to talk you into it. I'm not forcing anyone to download anything or to take my word for any of it.

All I can do is be straight with you about what these apps are, hold back the two that could do real damage until they've been properly checked, and leave the choice with you. Just offering a helping hand — take it or leave it.

— Shimo

Before you install

Windows will warn you

These apps aren't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows shows a blue "protected your PC" box. Click More info, then Run anyway. Your antivirus may complain for the same reason — a cleaner that deletes system files looks a lot like one that shouldn't.

Android needs permission once

Open the downloaded APK, and when your phone asks, allow installing from unknown sources. You only have to do this the first time.

CyberBuild needs a connection

It doesn't compile anything on your phone — it hands the work to GitHub's servers and brings the finished APK back. So it needs Wi-Fi or mobile data to do anything. The cleaners work fully offline.

Nothing phones home

No telemetry, no account, no ads. Settings stay on your machine.
