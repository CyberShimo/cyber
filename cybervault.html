Vault

Anything you keep is stored in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded. Paste a link you were given privately and it saves the same as any other — it just never becomes searchable by anyone else.

Settings
Unlocks name search and full channel listings. Get one from Google Cloud Console, enable YouTube Data API v3, then restrict it to your site's domain under HTTP referrers. Free tier is 10,000 units/day and a keyword search costs 100 of them.
